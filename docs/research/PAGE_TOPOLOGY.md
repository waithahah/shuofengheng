2. **Hero Banner Component (`src/components/HeroBanner.tsx`)**

4. **About Factory Section Component (`src/components/AboutSection.tsx`)**

5. **Top Products Component (`src/components/TopProducts.tsx`)**
   - Section Title: "Wholesale Top ranking Tableware"

6. **News & Articles Section Component (`src/components/NewsSection.tsx`)**

7. **Footer Component (`src/components/Footer.tsx`)**
